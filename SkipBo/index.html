Table Rules

  • Standard stock: 30 cards for 2–4 players; 20 for 5–6. Short game: 10 each.
  • Your hand fills to five at the start of your turn. Play all five to draw again.
  • Build each shared pile from 1 to 12. Skip-Bo cards are wild. Completed piles are recycled.
  • Play from your hand, stock top, or a discard top. Discard one hand card to end your turn.
  • Empty your stock to win the round: 25 points plus 5 per opponent stock card. First to 500 wins.
  • If no cards can be drawn, an empty-handed blocked player may pass. A fully blocked table redeals without points.
